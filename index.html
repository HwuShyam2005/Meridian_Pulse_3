Project 2

Meridian Pulse — Shipment Tracker Explainer

A 30-second data-visualization video across a real shipping route.

  • Supply-chain visibility is one of the most common content types logistics marketing teams publish — Maersk, DHL, and DP World all produce this genre.
  • Built a dot-matrix world map plotted at real port coordinates (Shanghai → Singapore → Suez → Rotterdam), an animated route, a delay-alert callout, and trust stats — no stock footage required.
  • Skills: data storytelling, geographic visualization, translating operational strengths into marketing content.
Project 3

Talking-Head Recut Treatment

A reusable overlay system for repackaging talking-head clips into social content.

  • Marketing teams constantly repackage interviews, webinars, and town halls into shareable clips — usually rebuilding the overlay design from scratch each time.
  • Built a standardized system: animated lower-third for name/title, kinetic captions timed to speech, and an isolated pull-quote moment — applies consistently to any future clip.
  • Skills: motion overlay design, content repurposing strategy, on-brand visual systems, social-first editing principles.
